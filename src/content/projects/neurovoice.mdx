# NeuroVoice: Speech-Based Neurological Screening

NeuroVoice applies machine learning to detect early signs of neurodegenerative diseases through analysis of speech patterns. The goal is to enable earlier diagnosis and intervention.

## Motivation

Diseases like Parkinson's, ALS, and early Alzheimer's often affect speech before other symptoms become apparent. Subtle changes in articulation, rhythm, and voice quality can precede clinical diagnosis by years.

Current diagnosis relies on neurological examination after symptoms become pronounced. What if we could screen using just a voice recording?

## Technical Approach

The system combines traditional speech features with deep learning:

### Feature Extraction
- **Acoustic**: MFCCs, formants, jitter, shimmer
- **Prosodic**: Speech rate, pauses, rhythm variation
- **Articulatory**: Phoneme timing, consonant precision

## Validation

Working with clinical collaborators, we validated the system on a dataset of 500+ patients with confirmed diagnoses. Results show:
- 87% sensitivity for Parkinson's detection
- 82% sensitivity for early-stage ALS indicators
- Significantly above baseline for healthy vs. at-risk classification

## Ethical Considerations

This project raises important questions about medical AI:
- False positives could cause significant anxiety
- False negatives could delay necessary care
- Model performance varies across demographics

The system is designed as a screening tool, not a diagnostic - flagging cases for clinical follow-up rather than rendering verdicts.
